*(Or: What a Space Ranger Toy Taught Me About Love)*

**"To infinity and beyond!"**

Every kid who grew up with Toy Story knows the line, arm shot straight
up, chin tilted like the ceiling was actually the sky. Buzz never once
explained how you get to infinity. He just said it like it was
obvious, like the going was the whole point, not the arriving.

That's stuck with me more as an adult than it ever did as a kid. Because
here's the thing about infinity nobody tells you: you don't get there
in one giant leap. You get there the boring way. Over and over. Same
launch pose, same day, again.

Which brings me to a plant on my desk and two words that sound way
more serious than they need to be.

**Idempotency** is just a fancy way of saying: do it again, and you get
the same good result. Water the plant today, tomorrow, the day after,
nothing breaks, nothing gets weird, it just keeps being watered. Say "I
love you" today, tomorrow, the day after; same deal. You don't need a
new, bigger declaration every time to make it count. The small
repeated thing IS the thing. Buzz didn't need a new catchphrase every
movie. He just said it again, and it worked every single time.

**Rate limiting** is the other half nobody wants to hear: you're not
supposed to do it all at once. Even Buzz doesn't blast off every five
minutes, that's not heroism, that's a battery problem. Pace matters.
Love, work, growing a garden, whatever you're chasing, burning
yourself out trying to prove you're serious right now, all at once,
just means you're not there tomorrow to keep showing up. Slow and
steady isn't the boring option. It's the only one that actually gets
you anywhere.

So what's next for me? Honestly, nothing dramatic. Just showing up
again. Same water, same plant, same "I love you," same code committed
one day at a time. No grand gesture required. Just repeat, don't rush,
trust the boring magic of doing the good thing again tomorrow.

How far would you go, for someone willing to say it again tomorrow,
exactly like they meant it today?

To infinity, and beyond.

~ Taz
