*(Or: What Freedom Actually Compiles To)*

**A fetter is a chain around the ankle, something built to stop you
mid-stride.**

I sat in a pew and heard that word for the first time in years, and
my mind, being what it is, went straight to code. We build systems
all day that are, in a sense, fettered on purpose, rate limits,
dependencies, locked schemas, guardrails against our own worst
instincts. Constraint has its place. But there's a difference between
a system that's constrained and a soul that's shackled, and the book
of Philippians is what finally showed me which one I'd been living
as.

Paul, writing from a prison cell, chained, literally, to a Roman
guard; doesn't write about his chains. He writes about what to think
about: whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right,
whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is admirable. A man in
actual fetters, describing an unfettered mind. That's not a coping
mechanism. That's a completely different operating system.

Here's what freedom in Christ actually looks like when you stop
treating it as a doctrine and start treating it as an architecture:

- **Unfettered Love**: - not love with a rate limit, not love that
  throttles when you've disappointed it one too many times.
- **Unfettered Peace**: -  the kind that doesn't depend on the build
  passing. Peace that holds even when the deploy fails.
- **Unfettered Provision**: - trusting the pipeline even when you
  can't see past the next commit.
- **Unfettered Contentment**: - Paul's own words: he'd learned to be
  content in any state. Not resigned. Content. There's a difference.
- **Unfettered Humility**: -no ego wrapped around the code, the
  title, the platform. Just open hands.
- **Unfettered Gain**: -  "to live is Christ, to die is gain",  the
  strange math where losing stops being loss.
- **Unfettered Joy**: -  joy that isn't a response to circumstances,
  but a root system underneath them.
- **Unfettered Perspective**: - seeing the whole system, not just the
  bug you're currently staring at.

I used to think career and calling were two different repos I had to
keep merging by hand. Merge conflict after conflict, late nights
reconciling who I was at church with who I was at a standup. But an
unfettered mind doesn't need two repos. It's one identity, deployed
everywhere it goes. The same person writing the pull request is the
same person who prayed that morning. No branch. No merge conflict.

So here's the question underneath all of it: what are you still
carrying that was never actually load-bearing? What chain have you
mistaken for structure?

Whatever is true. Whatever is honorable. Whatever is lovely. Think on
these things, and watch what unshackles.

Welcome to the digital garden.

~ Taz
